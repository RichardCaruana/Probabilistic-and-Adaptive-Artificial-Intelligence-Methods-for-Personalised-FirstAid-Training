which effects there airway,
breathing and circulation

2.2 BREATHING

•

Agonal gasps

•

Following a cardiac arrest,
40% of casualties present
agonal breathing.

•

They may be barely breathing
or taking noisy but infrequent
gasping.

•

Do not confuse this as ‘normal breathing’

•

A seizure like episode can occur

•

Do not confuse this with epilepsy.

•

If in doubt commence CPR
© AOFAQ 2017

2.2 GET HELP

Not breathing normally:
•

Ask helper to call emergency services or call
yourself if alone.

•

Try to stay with casualty.

•

Activate speaker function on phone to
communicate with dispatcher.

• Tell helper to inform emergency services that the casualty is
unresponsive and not breathing normally and give location of
casualty.
• Ensure helper has called the emergency services.
