4.2 CARDIOPULMONARY RESUSCITATION (CPR)

•

Following your primary survey, you have
established that the casualty is not
breathing

Undertake the following actions
▪

Ask helper to call emergency services (if
not already done so)

▪

Give 30 chest compressions
(compressing at 5-6 cm deep) at a rate of
100-120 per minute

▪

Followed by 2 rescue breaths

▪

Continue with 30 compressions, followed
by 2 rescue breaths

Infant and Child CPRfor information only
Follow adult sequence with the following amendments
If there are no signs of breathing after your 10 second look, listen and feel check, give children
and infants 5 initial rescue breaths. For infants place your mouth round the infants nose and
mouth to create a good seal. Take care not to over inflate.
If you are alone, perform CPR for 1 minute before going for help. 30 chest compressions to 2 rescue breaths.
Place two fingers in the centre of the infants chest, just below the nipple line compress the chest
to a depth of 1/3 at the same rate as adult and child, 100-120 per minute, in a sharp stabbing
motion.
Place one hand in the centre of the child’s chest. At the same
rate as adult chest compressions, compress the chest to a
depth of 1/3. Use two hands if unable to achieve compression
depth.

Infants (babies) are generally defined as aged from birth to one year; while children are
generally defined as aged from 1 year to the onset of puberty.
