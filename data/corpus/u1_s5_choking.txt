5.1 HOW TO IDENTIFY
A CASUALTY WHO IS CHOKING
•

Choking is caused by a foreign object which
becomes lodged in the airway. The severity of
the obstruction will be the determining factor in
applying the correct treatment.

There are two types of choking:
•

Partially blocked airway

•

Completely blocked airway

© AOFAQ 2017

90

5.1 IDENTIFY A CASUALTY WHO IS CHOKING

Partially blocked airway
•

The casualty is able to speak

•

The casualty is able to breath

•

The casualty is able to cough

•

The casualty will show
signs of distress

5.1 IDENTIFY A CASUALTY WHO IS CHOKING

Completely blocked airway
•

The casualty will be unable to speak

•

The casualty will be unable to breath

•

The casualty will be unable
to cough effectively

•

The casualty will show signs
of distress

•

The face will become red

•

Eyes will become enlarged

•

Grasping at the throat

5.2 ADMINISTER FIRST AID
TO A CHOKING CASUALTY

•Encourage coughing
•5 back blows
•5 Abdominal thrusts
•Repeat
•If casualty goes unconscious- start CPR
