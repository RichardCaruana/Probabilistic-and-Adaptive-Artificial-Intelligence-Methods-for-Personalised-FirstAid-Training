## Slide 103

8.1	IDENTIFY COMMON TRIGGERS 
FOR ANAPHYLAXIS 

This is the most severe form of 
an allergic reaction
It causes the immune system 
to over react to a threat
Reactions occur after an 
exposure to an allergen such
 as certain foods e.g. nuts, bee stings, 
insect bites, latex, medicines etc
Most casualty's with a history of anaphylaxis will carry an auto injector filled with adrenalin/epinephrine (commonly known as Ana-pen or Epi-pen)

## Slide 104

8.2 RECOGNISE ANAPHYLAXIS 

Rapid and exaggerated swelling of the face, lips, tongue and throat
Speaking becomes difficult and 
the voice becomes hoarse 
Difficulty in breathing
Red, blotchy and sometimes 
itchy skin
Extreme anxiety and 
a feeling of impending doom
Unconsciousness 

## Slide 105

8.3	ADMINISTER IMMEDIATE FIRST AID 
FOR A CASUALTY SUFFERING FROM ANAPHYLAXIS

Call 999/112
Lay the casualty in a comfortable position 
If the casualty is conscious assist 
them in administering 
the medication contained 
within the auto injector 
If the casualty is unconscious 
and not breathing normally 
administer the medication 
contained within the auto 
injector (only if trained) 
and begin CPR.
